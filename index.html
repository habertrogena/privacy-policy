This Privacy Policy is intended to satisfy Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1 (Data Collection and Storage), including disclosures related to face data and other sensitive personal information.

3. Face Data: Collection, Processing, Storage, Sharing, Retention, and Deletion

This section provides the specific face-data disclosures required for App Store Review and for users who upload facial photographs to SkinMap.

3.1 Why We Collect Face Data

We collect facial photographs only to:

  • Perform AI-powered skin analysis that you request
  • Generate personalized skincare recommendations
  • Enable you to view your previous scan history
  • Support consultations with skincare professionals when you choose to book that feature
  • Provide customer support related to your scans when you request help

We do not collect facial photographs for advertising, marketing, facial recognition, identity verification, or building biometric profiles.

3.2 How Face Data Is Processed

When you capture or upload a facial photograph, the processing flow is as follows:

  1. You capture a facial photograph with your camera or upload an image from your device.
  2. The image is transmitted securely over HTTPS and uploaded to Cloudflare R2 Object Storage.
  3. Cloudflare returns a secure object URL for the stored image.
  4. Our PostgreSQL database stores the Cloudflare object URL, scan metadata, and related account association. The raw image file itself is not stored inside the database.
  5. The image is processed by our AI analysis providers to generate skin analysis results and related recommendations:
    • Perfect Corp processes the facial photograph to perform skin analysis for the Service you request.
    • Google Gemini processes the facial photograph and/or related analysis inputs to generate or refine personalized skincare insights and recommendations for the Service you request.
  6. Analysis results are stored with your account so you can review them and receive recommendations.
  7. The facial photograph remains associated with your account until you delete the scan or delete your account, subject to the retention and deletion practices described below.

Perfect Corp and Google Gemini act as service providers / data processors on our behalf. Their processing is limited to providing the skin analysis and recommendation features you request. Facial photographs are not processed by Perfect Corp or Google Gemini for facial recognition, identity verification, advertising, marketing, or to build biometric profiles. We do not permit Perfect Corp or Google Gemini to use your facial photographs to train AI models for their own independent purposes.

The AI analysis is performed to evaluate skin-related characteristics for skincare purposes only. It is not used to recognize your identity, match your face against other faces, or create a biometric template.

3.3 Where Face Data Is Stored

  • Facial photographs: Stored in Cloudflare R2 Object Storage.
  • Database records: Our PostgreSQL database stores the secure image URL (Cloudflare object URL), scan metadata, and AI analysis results associated with your account. The database does not store the image binary itself.

3.4 Whether Face Data Is Shared

Facial photographs are:

  • Not sold
  • Not shared with advertisers
  • Not used for marketing
  • Not used for facial recognition
  • Not used to identify users across services
  • Not used to build biometric profiles
  • Not used to train AI models

Facial photographs may be processed only by infrastructure providers that supply storage and AI analysis necessary to perform the Service you request. These providers act as service providers / data processors on our behalf and are permitted to process face data only to provide the requested Service functions, not for their own independent purposes such as advertising or model training on your images.

Current third parties involved in face-image handling are:

  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare R2): Stores facial photographs securely. Our database stores only the Cloudflare object URL, not the image binary.
  • Perfect Corp: Receives and processes facial photographs to perform AI-powered skin analysis required for the Service.
  • Google Gemini: Receives and processes facial photographs and/or related analysis inputs to generate or refine personalized skincare insights and recommendations required for the Service.

Perfect Corp and Google Gemini process face data solely to generate the skin analysis results and recommendations you request. They are not permitted to sell your facial photographs, use them for advertising, use them for facial recognition, or use them to train AI models for their own independent purposes.

If you book a consultation with a skincare professional, we may share analysis results and other information you choose to provide for that consultation. Facial photographs are shared with a professional only if needed to deliver the consultation you request.

3.5 Face Data Retention Period

Facial photographs remain stored only while they are associated with your account and are needed to provide scan history and related Service features.

  • If you delete an individual scan, the associated facial photograph and related scan records are removed according to our deletion process.
  • If you delete your account, associated facial photographs are deleted from Cloudflare R2 storage, and related URLs, scan metadata, and analysis results are deleted from our systems, according to our deletion process.
  • We do not retain facial photographs indefinitely after account deletion for secondary uses such as marketing, advertising, facial recognition, or AI model training.

After a valid deletion request or account deletion is completed, we aim to permanently delete associated facial photographs and related personal data from active systems within 30 days, except where a limited copy must be retained temporarily for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or backup integrity. Any such limited retention is restricted to those purposes and is deleted when no longer required.

3.6 Face Data Deletion Process

You may delete face data as follows:

  • Delete a scan: Use the in-app controls to delete a previous scan. This removes the associated facial photograph from Cloudflare R2 and deletes related scan metadata and analysis results from our database according to our deletion process.
  • Delete your account: Use the in-app account deletion feature. Account deletion removes your account and associated facial photographs, image URLs, scan metadata, and analysis results according to our deletion process.
  • Request assistance: You may also email skinmapapk@gmail.com to request deletion of your account or specific scan data.

When a deletion is processed, we delete the corresponding object from Cloudflare R2 and remove or de-associate the related database records (image URL, scan metadata, and analysis results). Cached or backup copies, if any, are cleared or overwritten according to our backup rotation and deletion process.

3.7 Security Protections for Face Data

We apply the following protections to facial photographs and related data:

  • Images are transmitted over HTTPS.
  • Uploaded images are stored securely in Cloudflare R2.
  • Access to stored images is restricted to authorized backend services required to operate the Service.
  • Our database stores secure object URLs and analysis results, not unrestricted public image dumps.
  • We use access controls, secure transport, and service-provider safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We work to protect face data using industry-standard safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.
